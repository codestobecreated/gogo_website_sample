GETTING HERE

250 km from Chennai and 160 km from Bangalore, GOGO is an easy road-trip from either city. The campsite is connected by a paved highway, with the final stretch taking you into the hills.

BEST WINDOWS

October"March brings clear walking weather. If you're coming for the stars, keep an eye on the moon phase and weather forecast. Darker, clearer nights make for better stargazing.

PACK LIGHT, FOR THE OUTDOORS

Comfortable clothes, sturdy footwear, personal toiletries, sunscreen, insect repellent and a light jacket or extra layer are a good start. Bring a torch, power bank and any personal camping essentials you don't want to be without.
Camping gear: Bring your own setup or rent tents and selected equipment from GOGO

KNOW BEFORE

Mobile signal can be limited and there's no Wi-Fi, sort of the point. Expect uneven and rocky paths the campsite and surrounding terrain. The campsite to the forest boundary and the final 200 m park is rocky.
Come prepared for the outdoors, not a resort.

KIDS & PETS

Kids are welcome when accompanied by adults, and GOGO KT is pet-friendly. Just keep little explorers and four-legged campers supervised around uneven terrain, open areas, water and the forest boundary.

AT CAMP

Depending on your campsite, GOGO provides essentials including restrooms with showers, dining areas, kitchen facilities and campfire/BBQ spaces. Tents can be rented, and equipment such as kayaks, cycles and BBQ sets is also available.

Astro-Camping Guide

Stargazing at Vainu Bappu Highlands

The Vainu Bappu Observatory at Kavalur (Jawadhu Hills) houses Asia's historic large telescopes due to the region's exceptionally low atmospheric light pollution and high elevation.

🔭 Best Time for Milky Way: April to October during new moon phases.
🪐 Planet Watching: Jupiter, Saturn rings, and nebula binoculars provided at camp.
