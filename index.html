• THE PRACTICAL STUFF

Enough detail to arrive easy.

Getting Here

Drive from Chennai: ~4.5 hrs via Vellore. From Bangalore: ~3.5 hrs via Krishnagiri. Roads are paved all the way to camp. Free parking inside the property.

The Food

Fresh home-cooked local meals included: Saturday evening snacks, BBQ dinner, Sunday breakfast. Vegetarian & non-veg options. Filter coffee always brewing.

Tents & Washrooms

Clean waterproof dome tents with sleeping mats, blankets, and pillows. Clean, modern Western washrooms with running water (yes, really — clean toilets in the wild).

What to Bring

Warm layer (nights drop to 14–18°C), good walking shoes, flashlight/headlamp, personal toiletries, and an open mind. We provide tents, bedding, food, and activities.

Is it Safe?

Gated campsite with 24/7 caretakers. Safe for solo female campers, families with kids, and first-time campers. First-aid kit and local doctor on call.

Pets

Dogs love it here. Wide open spaces to run, swim in the lake, and sniff every tree in Jawadhu. Just let us know in advance so we can prepare.

Astro-Camping Guide

Stargazing at Vainu Bappu Highlands

The Vainu Bappu Observatory at Kavalur (Jawadhu Hills) houses Asia’s historic large telescopes due to the region's exceptionally low atmospheric light pollution and high elevation.

🔭 Best Time for Milky Way: April to October during new moon phases.
🪐 Planet Watching: Jupiter, Saturn rings, and nebula binoculars provided at camp.
